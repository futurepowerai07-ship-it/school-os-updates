Privacy Policy for School OS Pro

Application: School OS Pro (Future Power AI)
Effective Date: November 26, 2026
Core Privacy Commitment: School OS Pro is an offline-first desktop school management application. All institutional, student, staff, and fee records remain strictly on the user's local computer and their own authorized sync setup. We do not collect, access, rent, or sell any school or student data to third parties.

1. Information Handled by the Application

School OS Pro allows educational institutions to manage their own administrative records locally, including:

  • School profile details (name, address, logo, session, UDISE/school codes).
  • Student enrollment, attendance, fee receipts, transfer certificates (TC), and examination records.
  • Teacher, staff, transport route, and expense records.
  • Local photos and backup files saved inside the user-selected Local Media Vault folder on their computer.

All such data is entered, owned, and controlled solely by the school or authorized operator using the software.

2. Local Storage & Authorized Cloud/LAN Sync

  • Local Storage: By default, all application data is stored locally on the user's Windows device and backed up to the local folder chosen by the user (e.g., Local Media Vault).
  • Optional Sync: If the school administrator chooses to enable Multi-PC LAN Sync or authorized Cloud Sync (Firebase Realtime Database), records are synchronized strictly between the school's own licensed computers.
  • No External Tracking: The software does not contain advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, or third-party analytics.

3. Software Licensing & Activation

To prevent unauthorized copying and manage the 7-day trial or licensed Pro mode, School OS Pro generates a non-personal hardware identifier (Machine ID) locally on the computer. This identifier is used strictly for license validation and does not include personal browsing data, passwords, or student records.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Communication

  • We never sell, trade, or share any user or student data with marketers, advertisers, or external organizations.
  • Optional features such as generating PDF fee receipts or opening WhatsApp to send fee notifications/receipts to parents are initiated manually by the school operator directly from their device.

5. Data Security & User Control

Users have complete ownership and control over their records. They can export CSV reports, generate local backups, restore data, or permanently delete local application data from their computer at any time.

6. Children's Privacy

School OS Pro is an institutional desktop management tool intended solely for use by adult school administrators, principals, and authorized staff—not directly by children. Student records stored in the software remain under the exclusive control of the school.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect software improvements or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

8. Contact Information
